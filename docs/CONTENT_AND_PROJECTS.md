## 3. Project card copy rules

Each card should contain no more than:

- Category
- Project name
- 1 concise sentence describing the actual work
- View Project

Example structure:

```text
E-commerce
ZTS Fashion
Modern online store built for a fashion brand, with a responsive shopping experience and business-ready commerce flows.
View Project →
```

Do not add technologies to every card unless they materially help the visitor.

## 4. Case-study writing rules

A project page should answer:

1. What business/product was this for?
2. What problem needed solving?
3. What did Business Craft design/build?
4. What important capabilities were delivered?
5. What does the final experience look like?
6. What measurable result exists, if any?

If result metrics are unavailable, do not manufacture them. Replace “Results” with “What We Delivered” or “Outcome” and describe factual shipped functionality.

## 5. Testimonials

Only use a testimonial if:

- it came from a real client
- the wording is approved for publication
- attribution is accurate

Otherwise omit the testimonial section.

## 6. Contact information

No authoritative Business Craft public email, phone number, physical address, or social URLs are defined in this pack.

Implementation rule:

- keep contact data in one configuration object
- use clear placeholders in development
- do not render fake production contact data

## 7. Pricing

Do not publish pricing unless the owner explicitly asks for it in the live website scope. Pricing can evolve and should not be inferred from prior sales discussions.

## 8. Copy style

Prefer short, natural business English.

Examples:

- “Built around the way your business actually works.”
- “A better digital experience for your customers and your team.”
- “From first idea to launch, with one clear process.”

Avoid over-explaining technical details on primary marketing sections. Put technical detail in case studies where it supports credibility.
