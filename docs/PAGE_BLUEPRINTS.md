# Business Craft — Page Blueprints

## Global navigation

Recommended desktop navigation:

- Home
- Services
- Work
- About
- Contact
- CTA: Get a Free Consultation

Header should be visually quiet and integrate naturally into the hero on dark pages.

## 1. Homepage `/`

Reference: `../references/04-homepage-reference.png`

### Preferred section order

1. Header
2. Hero
3. Service overview
4. Featured work
5. Why Business Craft
6. Process
7. Proof / testimonials if verified
8. Final CTA
9. Footer

### Hero

Eyebrow:

**DIGITAL SOLUTIONS FOR REAL BUSINESS**

H1:

**Websites, E-commerce, Systems & Mobile Apps That Grow Your Business**

Supporting copy:

**We design and build digital products that help businesses sell more, work smarter and grow faster.**

Actions:

- Get a Free Consultation
- See Our Work

Visual: real Business Craft project shown across laptop/phone or another realistic multi-device composition.

### Stats row

The design reference contains stats. Do **not** show stats until each number is verified. It is acceptable to remove this row or replace it with non-numeric trust signals such as:

- Websites
- E-commerce
- Systems
- Mobile Apps

### Services

Four cards:

- Websites — modern, fast, SEO-friendly business websites
- E-commerce — online stores designed to sell and scale
- Systems — custom systems built around business workflows
- Mobile Apps — iOS/Android or cross-platform product experiences

### Featured work

Prioritize real projects. Cards should show:

- project image
- project name
- category
- concise factual description
- View Project

### Why Business Craft

Suggested pillars:

- Business Focus
- Modern & Scalable
- End-to-End Support
- Clear Communication

Do not claim support/service levels that cannot actually be delivered.

### Process

Suggested four-step version:

1. Discover
2. Plan
3. Build
4. Launch

A five-step variant can include Design or Testing separately.

## 2. Services overview `/services`

No dedicated approved screenshot is included yet; derive it from the homepage and approved design system.

### Structure

1. Hero: “Digital Solutions Built Around Your Business”
2. Four primary service blocks
3. Outcomes / what each service solves
4. Shared process
5. Selected work mapped to services
6. Why Business Craft

## 3. Work / Case Studies `/work`

Reference: `../references/05-work-case-studies-reference.png`

### Structure

1. Dark hero: “Real Projects. Real Results.”
2. Optional proof summary — verified facts only
3. Filter bar
4. Featured project grid
5. Additional projects
6. Industries/capabilities row if useful
7. CTA
8. Footer

### Filters

- All
- Websites
- E-commerce
- Systems
- Mobile Apps

Use client-side filtering without a page reload unless the project count becomes large.

Do not show invented project results. A portfolio is credible without fake metrics.

## 4. Project detail `/work/[slug]`

Reference: `../references/07-zts-project-detail-reference.png`

The ZTS mockup demonstrates the template, not a mandate to publish every visible claim.

### Structure

1. Breadcrumb
2. Project type label
3. Project title
4. Factual one-line summary
5. Longer intro paragraph
6. Project hero visual
7. Verified metadata
8. Screenshot gallery
9. About the project
10. Key features / delivered capabilities
11. Results — only if measured and verified
12. Testimonial — only if real and approved
13. Related projects
14. CTA/footer

### Metadata

Possible fields:

- Category
- Market
- Platform/technology
- Launch status

Do not force a completion year or location if unknown.

### Screenshot gallery

For e-commerce, useful screens include:

- Home
- Listing/category
- Product detail
- Cart/checkout
- Mobile
- Admin

For systems, use workflow/dashboard/report screens instead.

## 5. About `/about`

### Goal

Explain how Business Craft works and why a business should trust it without pretending to be a larger organization than it is.

### Structure

1. Hero: practical positioning statement
2. What Business Craft does
3. Principles / how work is approached
4. Capabilities
5. Process
6. Selected work
7. Founder/team section only if real content is available
8. CTA

Do not use generic fake team portraits.

## 6. Contact / Get a Quote `/contact`

### Structure

1. Clear consultation headline
2. Short expectations paragraph
3. Contact form
4. Direct contact channels once supplied
5. FAQ / response-time note only if factual

### Recommended form fields

- Name
- Company / Brand (optional)
- Email
- Phone / WhatsApp (optional depending on desired workflow)
- Service
- Budget range (optional)
- Project brief

Use a success state and robust validation.

Do not hardcode fake phone/email/address values from mockups.

## 7. Service detail — Websites `/services/websites`

Reference: `../references/06-websites-service-reference.png`

### Structure

1. Breadcrumb
2. Hero
3. Core benefits
4. Deliverables
5. Process
6. Featured website projects
7. Technologies only if genuinely used/relevant
8. Suitable business types
9. FAQ
10. CTA

Preferred hero heading:

**Websites That Represent Your Business**

Supporting idea:

Modern, fast, responsive and SEO-friendly websites designed around business goals.

## 8. Service detail — E-commerce `/services/ecommerce`

Reuse the same design grammar as Websites, but content must focus on selling online.

Suggested capabilities:

- catalog and categories
- product variants
- cart and checkout
- payment integration
- shipping integration
- inventory/order workflow
- mobile-first storefront
- admin operations

Do not promise a specific payment/shipping provider as a universal capability unless it is actually offered for that project/market.

## 9. Service detail — Systems `/services/systems`

Focus on operations rather than generic software buzzwords.

Possible content:

- custom business workflows
- dashboards
- roles and permissions
- reporting
- inventory/operations
- integrations
- automation
- auditability

Show system/dashboard project screenshots where available.

## 10. Service detail — Mobile Apps `/services/mobile-apps`

Focus on product experience and real use cases.

Possible content:

- customer-facing apps
- internal business apps
- cross-platform builds
- APIs/integrations
- authentication
- notifications
- analytics where applicable

Avoid claiming both native iOS and Android engineering if the actual offering is cross-platform only; describe the real delivery model.

## 11. Footer

Recommended groups:

- Brand + short descriptor
- Quick Links
- Services
- Social / contact links once confirmed

Legal line should use the **current year dynamically**, not the `2024` placeholder visible in concept art.
