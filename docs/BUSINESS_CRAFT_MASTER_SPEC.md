# Business Craft — Master Specification

## 1. Project summary

Business Craft is a digital-solutions brand focused on helping real businesses launch, sell, operate, and grow through well-designed software products.

The website must position Business Craft as a capable, professional digital partner — not as a generic freelancer portfolio and not as a flashy AI/startup landing page.

### Core services

1. Websites
2. E-commerce
3. Systems
4. Mobile Apps

### Brand descriptor

**Digital Solutions for Real Business**

### Preferred homepage message

**Websites, E-commerce, Systems & Mobile Apps That Grow Your Business**

Supporting copy:

> We design and build digital products that help businesses sell more, work smarter and grow faster.

Primary CTA: **Get a Free Consultation**  
Secondary CTA: **See Our Work**

## 2. Positioning

Business Craft should feel:

- professional
- established
- practical
- modern
- premium without being expensive-looking for its own sake
- technical without being developer-centric
- confident without exaggerated claims

The visitor should quickly understand that Business Craft can handle complete business-facing digital work, from a marketing website to an e-commerce store, custom internal system, or mobile app.

## 3. Audience

Primary audience:

- small and medium businesses
- retail brands
- online brands
- service businesses
- businesses that need custom internal systems
- businesses upgrading from social-media-only selling to a proper digital product

The site can also support GCC outreach, so the visual quality must be high enough to present Business Craft as a serious agency/technology partner rather than a low-cost template provider.

## 4. Language

The Business Craft website is **English only** for the current implementation.

Do not add Arabic, RTL, language switchers, or bilingual duplication unless explicitly requested later.

## 5. Approved brand direction

The chosen direction is:

**Minimal / Premium / Editorial**

Visual characteristics:

- deep navy / near-black hero areas
- crisp off-white/light content sections
- restrained electric blue accent
- large, direct typography
- generous whitespace
- realistic device/product mockups
- clean product UI screenshots
- subtle lines and borders
- restrained corners and shadows
- deliberate asymmetry where useful, but never messy

Avoid:

- loud gradients as the main identity
- purple/pink startup aesthetics
- excessive glassmorphism
- futuristic neon UI
- 3D abstract blobs as the core visual language
- generic AI-generated people as filler
- excessive motion
- over-rounded cards everywhere
- giant decorative icons with little meaning
- “we are innovators disrupting...” copy

## 6. Visual identity

### Colors

- Primary Navy: `#0B1320`
- Secondary Charcoal: `#1F2937`
- Accent Blue: `#3B82F6`
- Gray: `#94A3B8`
- Light: `#F8FAFC`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

### Typography

Primary family: **Inter**

The typography should be compact, clean, and businesslike. Headings may be tightly tracked; body text should retain comfortable reading width and line-height.

### Logo

Approved direction:

- geometric B monogram
- Business Craft wordmark
- works on dark and light backgrounds
- monogram can be used alone for favicon/social avatar

The PNG reference is the visual direction. Do not trace a low-quality raster logo into production if proper vector assets become available later; swap to the authoritative vector asset when supplied.

## 7. Tone of voice

Tone:

- clear
- specific
- confident
- practical
- commercially aware

Prefer:

- “Custom systems built around your workflow.”
- “E-commerce stores designed to sell and scale.”
- “Fast, modern websites built around your business.”

Avoid:

- “We revolutionize your digital future.”
- “Cutting-edge AI-powered synergy.”
- fake scarcity or fake urgency
- inflated numeric claims without proof

## 8. Content integrity

Generated design references contain illustrative content. Treat the following as **unverified unless independently confirmed**:

- 50+ projects delivered
- 30+ happy clients
- 98% client satisfaction
- 10+ industries served
- +120% sales increases
- 10,000+ products sold
- 50% faster processing
- named testimonials and star ratings
- completion years shown in mockups
- phone numbers, emails, addresses, or social links shown in mockups

Do not publish any of these by default.

If a section requires social proof and verified testimonials are unavailable, use real project evidence instead: project screenshots, live links, capabilities, deliverables, and factual descriptions.

## 9. Website goals

Primary goals:

1. Make the services immediately understandable.
2. Build credibility using real project work.
3. Convert suitable visitors into consultations/inquiries.
4. Make Business Craft look consistent across web and future social content.
5. Create a reusable system so future projects/services can be added without redesigning the site.

## 10. Conversion strategy

Primary conversion path:

Home → Services/Work → relevant project or service → consultation CTA → Contact/Get a Quote form.

Use one dominant CTA label site-wide: **Get a Free Consultation**.

Secondary CTA: **See Our Work** or **View Project**, depending on context.
